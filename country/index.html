Country guides

Universities, costs, work rights and visa essentials for each destination.

Australia

World-class universities and TAFE institutes, a relaxed lifestyle and a three-year Indian degree accepted for many master's programmes.

Canada

Colleges and universities known for practical, job-focused programmes, co-op placements and a clear route to work after graduation.

United Kingdom

Historic universities, one-year master's degrees and a short flight home. A strong choice if you want a recognised degree quickly.

Europe

Germany, France, Ireland, Italy, Poland and more, with lower tuition and many programmes taught in English.

New Zealand

Eight well-ranked universities, polytechnics and private institutes in one of the safest and most welcoming countries to study.

USA

The widest choice of universities in the world, from Ivy League research universities to community colleges, with strong options in STEM.

Asia

Singapore, the UAE, Japan, Malaysia, South Korea and more: world-ranked universities closer to home.
