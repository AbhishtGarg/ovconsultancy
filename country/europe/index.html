Why study in Europe

Europe offers world-class universities, many programmes taught in English, and tuition that is often far lower than in the UK, the USA or Australia. A residence permit from one Schengen country also lets you travel to most others for short trips.

Each country has its own visa rules, costs and work rights. Choose one of our most popular European destinations below to see the essentials.

Study in Germany

Strong engineering, technology and business programmes, with many master's degrees taught in English.

Main intakes
Winter (Oct) and Summer (Apr)
Work during study
140 full days or 280 half days a year, or up to 20 hrs/week
Funds to show
€992 a month (€11,904 a year) in a blocked account
After graduation
18 months to look for skilled work, taking any job meanwhile
  • Most public universities charge little or no tuition beyond a semester fee
  • Funds are shown through a blocked account (Sperrkonto)
  • German graduates can later move to a settlement permit

Study in France

Business schools and universities with English-taught master's programmes, and special post-study rights for Indian graduates.

Main intakes
Sep (main), Jan–Feb
Work during study
Up to 964 hours a year (about 20 hrs/week)
Funds to show
€877.50 a month for applications from 1 Aug 2026 (previously €615)
After graduation
APS post-study permit: 12 months, up to 24 months for Indian master's graduates
  • Indian students apply through Campus France (Études en France) before the visa
  • Long-stay student visa (VLS-TS) also acts as your residence permit
  • Many grandes écoles teach business and engineering in English

Study in Ireland

An English-speaking EU country with a strong tech and pharma job market and a clear post-study route.

Main intakes
Sep (main), Jan–Feb
Work during study
20 hrs/week in term; 40 hrs/week Jun–Sep and 15 Dec–15 Jan
Funds to show
€10,000 a year for courses longer than 8 months
After graduation
Stamp 1G: 12 months after a bachelor's (level 8), up to 24 months after a master's
  • Your course must be on the Interim List of Eligible Programmes (ILEP) for Stamp 2 work rights
  • Register for your Irish Residence Permit (IRP) after you arrive
  • Ireland is not in the Schengen area, so it has its own visa

Study in Netherlands

Research universities and universities of applied sciences with hundreds of English-taught programmes.

Main intakes
Sep (main), Feb
Work during study
16 hrs/week, or full-time in Jun–Aug (your employer needs a TWV permit)
Funds to show
€1,130.77 a month in 2026 (about €13,570 a year)
After graduation
Orientation year (zoekjaar): up to 1 year to find work
  • Your university applies for your visa and residence permit for you
  • Funds are usually paid to the university before the application
  • During the orientation year you can work without a separate work permit

Study in Italy

Historic public universities with low tuition, English-taught degrees and regional scholarships.

Main intakes
Sep–Oct (main), some Feb
Work during study
20 hrs/week, up to 1,040 hours a year
Funds to show
€10,179.85 a year for 2026–27 entry
After graduation
12-month job-seeking permit (attesa occupazione)
  • Pre-enrol on the Universitaly portal before your visa appointment
  • Apply for your residence permit (permesso di soggiorno) within 8 working days of arrival
  • Many regions offer income-based scholarships and fee waivers

Study in Spain

Affordable cities, a growing range of English-taught programmes, and some of Europe's most generous student work rights.

Main intakes
Sep–Oct (main), some Feb
Work during study
Up to 30 hrs/week for higher-education students
Funds to show
€600 a month (€7,200 a year), based on IPREM
After graduation
Job-search permit after an official degree
  • Tuition money doesn't count towards the funds you must show
  • Work rights come automatically with your student residence card for higher education
  • Private health insurance is required

Study in Poland

Affordable EU degrees, low living costs and a growing tech sector in Warsaw, Kraków and Wrocław.

Main intakes
Oct (main), Feb
Work during study
Allowed without a separate work permit while you study full-time
Funds to show
PLN 1,010 a month, plus housing costs and PLN 2,500 for the trip home
After graduation
9-month permit to look for work, granted once
  • Show proof of accommodation along with your funds
  • Apply for a temporary residence card before your visa expires
  • Public universities offer many English-taught programmes

Study in Malta

An English-speaking EU country with a Mediterranean lifestyle and degrees aligned to the European framework.

Main intakes
Oct (main), Feb
Work during study
Up to 20 hrs/week after your first 3 months, with a Jobsplus licence
Funds to show
At least 75% of the national minimum wage for each month of study
After graduation
9-month post-study stay to look for work
  • English is an official language, so classes and daily life are in English
  • You need a type D visa first, then a residence permit after arrival
  • Health insurance is mandatory

Study in Cyprus

Affordable tuition and English-taught degrees at public and private universities in a safe, sunny EU country.

Main intakes
Sep–Oct (main), Jan–Feb
Work during study
Up to 20 hrs/week in term and 38 hrs in holidays, in approved sectors
Funds to show
Shown with bank statements or a bank guarantee; amount set by the Migration Department
After graduation
No dedicated post-study visa; staying on usually needs a job offer and work permit
  • Private universities teach in English and public universities offer English-taught courses too
  • Register with the police Alien Branch soon after arrival
  • Work contracts must be approved by the Department of Labour

English tests for Europe

For English-taught programmes, universities across Europe usually accept IELTS or PTE. Some programmes are taught in the local language and need a language certificate instead. We'll check what each of your shortlisted programmes asks for.
