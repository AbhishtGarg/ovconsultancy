Why study in Australia

Australian universities, vocational colleges and TAFE institutes are well regarded internationally, and the government closely monitors education standards. Many master's programmes accept a three-year Indian degree, and cities like Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane and Perth are popular with Indian students.

The student visa (subclass 500)

From 1 July 2026 the visa application charge is AUD 2,500. You need an English score (an overall IELTS 6.0 or equivalent for most courses) and must meet the Genuine Student requirement by explaining why you chose your course and how it fits your background and goals.

Costs and proof of funds

You must show at least AUD 29,710 a year for living costs, on top of tuition and travel. International students also need Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the whole of their visa; Australia's Medicare scheme does not cover them.

Working while you study

Students can work up to 48 hours a fortnight while their course is in session and unlimited hours during scheduled breaks. Students on a master's by research or a PhD have no work limit.

After you graduate

The Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) lets you stay and work after your studies: generally 2 years after a bachelor's or coursework master's and 3 years after a research master's or PhD.

Facts reviewed November 2026. Visa rules change often; a counsellor will confirm the current rules for your case.

Common questions

Is IELTS 6.0 enough for Australia?

An overall 6.0 meets the general student visa requirement, but your university may ask for more, especially for nursing, teaching and some postgraduate courses.

What is the Genuine Student requirement?

A written explanation of why you are studying this course in Australia, how it connects to your past studies and future plans, and that you will follow your visa conditions. We help you write it.
