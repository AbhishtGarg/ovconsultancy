What is IELTS?

IELTS (International English Language Testing System) is an English test for people who want to study or work where English is the main language. Universities in the UK, Australia, New Zealand, the USA and Canada accept it for admission.

It tests the four skills, listening, reading, writing and speaking, and comes in two versions. Students take IELTS Academic. People who need a score for work or migration take IELTS General Training.

Common questions

Academic or General Training?

If you are applying to a university, take Academic. General Training is for work and migration applications.

Paper or computer?

The test content and scoring are the same. Some students find typing the writing tasks faster; others prefer paper. We help you decide.

How long is my score valid?

Most universities accept IELTS results for two years from the test date.
