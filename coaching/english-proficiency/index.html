The course

Focus
Speaking and pronunciation

Speak English with confidence

Clear pronunciation and confident spoken English help in visa interviews, at university and at work. Our English proficiency course focuses on speaking, so you can handle interviews and everyday conversations with ease.

What you practise

  • Pronunciation and clear speech
  • Everyday conversation: introductions, questions, phone calls
  • Vocabulary for study, travel and work
  • Listening to different accents
  • Speaking in interviews and presentations

Who it is for

Students preparing to study abroad, anyone who wants to feel more confident before an IELTS or PTE speaking test, and working professionals who use English at work.
