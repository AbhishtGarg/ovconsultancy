Privacy policy

How we collect and use the details you share with us through this website.

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Offshore Vistas Consultancy (OVC), with branches in Kurukshetra and Ladwa, Haryana. If you have questions about this policy, email info@ovconsultancy.com or call (+91)8749961000.

What we collect

When you fill in our registration or call-back form, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, city, education, preferred branch, the service and country you are interested in, and any message you write.

We do not ask for passport numbers, bank details or other sensitive documents through this website.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry and the services you asked about
  • To arrange counselling, coaching or visa guidance you choose to take up
  • To keep a record of your enquiry so we can follow up

We do not sell your details or share them with third parties for their own marketing.

Services we use

Our forms are processed by Google, which stores your submission in our Google account and sends it to us by email. Maps on our Contact page are provided by Google Maps, and the WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp. These services have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to help you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time by emailing info@ovconsultancy.com.

Questions about your data?

Email or call us and we will help.
