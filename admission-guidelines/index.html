Getting into a university abroad

We guide you through how admissions abroad work: which courses suit your studies, what score you need and how to present your application well. Our counsellors then help you prepare and submit each application.

Documents we help you prepare

  • Statement of purpose (SOP). Your reasons for the course, the country and your career plan, in your own words.
  • Letters of recommendation (LOR). Guidance on who to ask and what a strong letter covers.
  • Resume. A clean, international-style CV for universities that ask for one.
  • Academic documents. Marksheets, transcripts and certificates, checked for completeness.

Application timeline

  1. 10 to 12 months before your intake

    Counselling, shortlist and English test preparation.

  2. 8 to 9 months before

    Write your SOP, collect letters and submit applications.

  3. 5 to 6 months before

    Receive offers, accept one and arrange funds.

  4. 3 to 4 months before

    File your visa and plan accommodation.

Deadlines vary. Some universities close applications early for popular courses. Your counsellor will give you the exact dates for your shortlist.

Common questions

What score do I need for admission?

It depends on the course and university. Many ask for an overall IELTS band of 6.0 to 6.5 or an equivalent PTE score, with minimum scores in each section.

Can I apply with a study gap?

Often, yes. A gap is easier to explain when you can show what you did during it, such as work experience or a course. We help you present it honestly in your SOP.

How many universities should I apply to?

Usually three to five, mixing one ambitious choice with options where your profile is a strong match.
