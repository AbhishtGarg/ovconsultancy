Who we are

Offshore Vistas Consultancy (OVC) is an overseas education consultancy. We take care of every part of studying abroad, from choosing the right country and course to filing your visa, so students and parents have one team to rely on instead of several.

What we do

  • Career counselling. Shortlisting countries, courses and universities that fit your profile and goals.
  • Admissions. Preparing and submitting applications, with guidance on your SOP, letters and resume.
  • English test coaching. IELTS and PTE classes with practice tests, plus spoken English and personality development.
  • Study visas. Forms, documents and interview preparation, checked against the current rules.
  • Pre-departure seminars. What to expect at the visa interview and in your first weeks abroad.

How we work

We choose courses for where they lead, not just for admission. We explain each step so you understand your own file. And because our branches are local, you can walk in and talk to the person handling your case.
