Complete visa assistance

We give complete study visa assistance: filling in the application form, getting your documents ready and preparing you for the interview where one is required. Requirements vary from country to country and change often, so we check your file against the current rules before it is submitted.

Proof of funds at a glance

Each country sets its own living-cost amount, on top of your tuition fees.

CountryLiving costs you must show
AustraliaAUD 29,710 a year
CanadaCAD 23,448 for a single student (applications from 1 September 2026), plus first-year tuition and travel
UK£1,529 a month in London or £1,171 outside London, for up to 9 months (rising to £1,570 and £1,203 from 30 November 2026)
New ZealandNZD 20,000 a year (NZD 1,667 a month for shorter courses)
USANo fixed government amount; your university sets the minimum on your I-20

Facts reviewed November 2026. Visa rules change often; a counsellor will confirm the current rules for your case.

Interview preparation

Some countries, including the USA, interview every student visa applicant in person. We run mock interviews so you can explain your course, your funding and your plans clearly and confidently.
