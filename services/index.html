How a typical file moves

Every student is different, but most files follow these five stages.

  1. Counselling and shortlist

    We review your academics, budget and goals, and shortlist countries and courses.

  2. English test

    You prepare for IELTS or PTE with us and book the test when your practice scores are ready.

  3. Applications

    We prepare your SOP and documents and submit applications to your shortlisted universities.

  4. Visa file

    Once you have an offer, we prepare your visa file against the current rules for your country.

  5. Pre-departure

    A seminar on travel, arrival and your first weeks abroad.
