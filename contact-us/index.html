Before you visit

A few things that make your first counselling session more useful.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but calling ahead on (+91)8749961000 means a counsellor will be free when you arrive.

What should I bring?

Your 10th and 12th marksheets, graduation documents if you have them, any IELTS or PTE score, and your passport if you have one. Photos on your phone are fine for a first meeting.

Can my parents come along?

Yes, and we encourage it. Decisions about studying abroad usually involve the whole family, and we are happy to answer their questions too.

Fill in the registration form and a counsellor will call you back.
