Common questions

Something else on your mind? Call or WhatsApp us and a counsellor will answer.

Should I take IELTS or PTE?

Both are widely accepted by universities in all the countries we work with. PTE is fully computer-based with fast results; IELTS has a face-to-face speaking test. Check what your chosen university accepts, then pick the format that suits you. We coach for both and can help you decide after a practice test.

What score do I need?

It depends on the course and the university. Many undergraduate and postgraduate courses ask for an overall IELTS band of 6.0 to 6.5 with no section below a set minimum. Competitive courses ask for more. Your counsellor will tell you the exact score for each option on your shortlist.

How early should I start?

Ideally 8 to 12 months before your intake. That leaves time to prepare for the English test, apply to universities, receive an offer and file your visa without rushing.

Which branch should I visit?

Either one. We have branches in Kurukshetra and Ladwa, and the same team supports your file whichever one you walk into. Call us to book a time.
